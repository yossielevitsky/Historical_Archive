Wiznitz

ויז׳ניץ

Contenu principal

La synagogue Wiznitz est au service des adeptes locaux de la dynastie hassidique de Vizhnitz.

Tradition

Vizhnitz is renowned for the warmth of its prayers and its emphasis on "Ahavas Yisrael" (love for fellow Jews).
