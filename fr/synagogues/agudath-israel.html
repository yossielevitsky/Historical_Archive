Agudath Israël

אגודת ישראל

The Agudath Israel synagogue has served as a political and religious center for the Agudath Israel movement in Antwerp.

Histoire

Established in the 1930s on Mercatorstraat, it has been a focal point for Orthodox Jewish activism and community organization.
