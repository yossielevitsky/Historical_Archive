Antwerp Names Project

In memory of the Antwerp victims of the Holocaust

This database contains the names of thousands of Antwerp victims of the Holocaust. The data is based on the Antwerp Names Project and is made available for research, education, and remembrance.

Historical Context

The Antwerp Names Project documents the Antwerp victims of persecution during the Second World War. The project contributes to preserving their memory and supports historical research into the Jewish community of Antwerp.

Source Attribution

Source: FelixArchief Antwerp – Antwerp Names Project. Data based on an export of June 23, 2026. This data represents a snapshot; records may have been modified or supplemented afterwards.
