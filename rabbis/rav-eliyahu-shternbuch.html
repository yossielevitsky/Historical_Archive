Rav Eliyahu Shternbuch

הרב אליהו שטרנבוך

Overview: Rabbi Eliyahu Sternbuch zt”l was the Av Beis Din of the Machzikei Hadas community in Antwerp and one of the most respected halachic authorities in Belgium for over half a century. Known for his humility, deep Torah knowledge, and lifelong dedication to communal service, he played a central role in strengthening Orthodox Jewish life in Antwerp after the war.

Early Life and Education

Rabbi Eliyahu Sternbuch zt”l was born in 1931 in London, England, to Rabbi Asher Sternbuch and Rebbetzin Devorah Sternbuch.

In his youth, he studied at the Gateshead Yeshiva in England and later at the Chevron Yeshiva in Jerusalem, where he became known for his diligence, sharp analytical ability, and seriousness in Torah study.

After his marriage to Rebbetzin Miriam (daughter of the Seret-Vizhnitz dynasty), he became connected to prominent rabbinic families and further developed his path in communal leadership.

Early Rabbinic Career

Rabbi Sternbuch zt”l served as a ram at Yeshivas Yacheil Yisroel–Vizhnitz in Haifa, where he influenced students through his clarity in halacha and emphasis on yiras Shamayim.

His reputation as a serious Torah scholar and capable communal leader led to his appointment in one of the most significant rabbinic positions in Western Europe.

Chief Rabbi of Machzikei Hadas Antwerp

Rabbi Sternbuch zt”l was appointed Rav and Av Beis Din of the Machzikei Hadas community in Antwerp, Belgium. He served in this role for approximately 54 years, making him one of the longest-serving rabbinic leaders in modern European Jewish history.

During his tenure, he became a central figure in Antwerp’s Orthodox infrastructure, overseeing halachic rulings, communal standards, and religious life. His leadership helped shape the stability and growth of the community over decades.

He was widely respected not only for his Torah knowledge, but also for his accessibility and compassion. Many community members recall his willingness to listen and his consistent efforts to assist individuals in both spiritual and personal matters.

Family Connections

Rabbi Sternbuch zt”l was part of a prominent rabbinic family. His brother was Rabbi Moshe Sternbuch, one of the leading halachic authorities in Jerusalem and Gaavad of the Eidah HaChareidis.

Through his extended family, he was connected to several major rabbinic figures across Europe and Israel, including his brother Rabbi Moshe Sternbuch and his father-in-law, Rabbi Yisachar Dov Hager of Seret-Vizhnitz, reflecting the wide influence of the Sternbuch dynasty.

Passing

Rabbi Eliyahu Sternbuch zt”l passed away in 2017 at the age of 86. His passing marked the end of an era in Antwerp’s rabbinic leadership, where he had served as a stabilizing and guiding figure for more than half a century.

Legacy

Rabbi Sternbuch zt”l is remembered as one of the foundational rabbinic leaders of post-war Antwerp. His decades of service strengthened the Machzikei Hadas community and helped maintain a strong Orthodox infrastructure in Belgium.

His legacy continues through his family, his students, and the institutions he helped shape.
