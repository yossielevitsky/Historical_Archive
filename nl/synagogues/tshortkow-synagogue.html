Tshortkow

טשורטקוב

The Tshortkow Synagogue continues the traditions of the Chortkov (Tshortkow) Hasidic dynasty.

Heritage

Rooted in the regal Hasidic tradition of Ruzhin, the Tshortkow community maintains a dignified and royal atmosphere in its prayer services.

At a Glance

Type Hasidic Dynasty
Lineage Ruzhin
