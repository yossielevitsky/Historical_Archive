Satmar-synagoge

בית המדרש סאטמאר

De Satmar-synagoge is de spirituele thuisbasis van de Satmar-chassidische gemeenschap in Antwerpen.

Gemeenschap

Known for its strict adherence to tradition and strong anti-Zionist stance, the Satmar community is one of the largest and most influential in the Hasidic world, with a significant presence in Antwerp.
