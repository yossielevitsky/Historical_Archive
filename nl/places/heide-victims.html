Heide-Kalmthout Holocaust Victims

In memory of the Jewish residents of Heide (Kalmthout) who perished in the Holocaust

Out of approximately 230 Jewish residents registered ("ingeschreven") in Kalmthout at the outbreak of the war, 40 were murdered in the Holocaust. This list records the names and family groupings of those victims from Heide, Kalmthout.
